About Us

We Treat Every Pet Like Our Own

Hi! I'm Mrs. Rutledge, and I've been caring for pets in our community for years. What started as helping a few neighbors has grown into a trusted pet sitting service built on one simple belief — every animal deserves safe, loving, attentive care.

We keep things personal and small so your pet always gets one-on-one attention. No big kennels, no cages — just a warm, happy home environment where pets can truly relax and thrive.

  • 🏠 Home-based care — no cold kennels
  • 💉 Updates & photos sent to you daily
  • 🚶 Walks, play time & plenty of cuddles
  • 📞 Always reachable by text or call
